## Key Objections

**Q: How many sellers do you have?**
A: We're onboarding our founding 100 right now.
   Topgun Maintenance LLC is our first verified merchant.
   Founding sellers pay $0 during the founding period.

**Q: How do you make money?**
A: Flat monthly tiers after the founding period —
   $29/$49/$99/month for sellers.
   On-site advertising for featured placements.
   AI social media service for Pro sellers.
   AI retention program replacing direct mail.
   1% cut on buyer transactions.
   White-label loyalty for enterprises.

**Q: Why not just use Whatnot?**
A: Whatnot takes $800+ on every $10k/month in sales.
   We take $29/month no matter how much you sell.
   Plus we have loyalty built in, AI retention,
   and we don't exist yet on local services — Whatnot doesn't
   help a mobile detailer or handyman find customers.

**Q: Why not just use Google Maps?**
A: Google Maps shows your address. DUM Club shows your
   best deal this week, your live stream, your reviews,
   and lets customers book and pay in one place.
   Google charges $500-$2,000/month in ads to be seen.
   DUM Club charges $29/month flat.

**Q: What about loyalty — do you compete with Yotpo?**
A: Yotpo charges $199-$999/month for loyalty only.
   We charge $29/month and loyalty is built into every tier.
   Plus ours works cross-merchant — earn at the detailer,
   spend at the pizza shop. Nobody else does that.
