# Judge Demo Script — DUM Club

## Opening (5 seconds)

"DUM Club turns any idea into a live business in 60 seconds. Real storefront, real payments, real AI sales assistant."

## Demo (60 seconds)

### Step 1: Type an idea (10s)
Open dum.club. Type: "A mobile car wash and detailing service with three tiers"
Point out: live preview appears as you type.

### Step 2: Launch (20s)
Click "Start Selling →"
Sign in with Google (one click).
Watch AI generation progress: "Building your storefront... Creating offers... Connecting payments..."

### Step 3: Show the business (10s)
Land on project page. Point out:
- Real business name (AI-generated)
- Real offers with real prices
- Stripe checkout ready

### Step 4: AI Sales Assistant (15s)
Click "Ask [Business Name]" button.
Type: "Which package should I pick?"
AI responds with specific offer recommendation and price.
Click the offer name in the response → scrolls to offers.

### Step 5: Rewards (5s)
"Every purchase earns DUM Points. Customers use them for discounts at any business on the platform."

## Closing (5 seconds)

"Shopify gives you tools. DUM Club gives you a business. 60 seconds from idea to selling."

## Key talking points for Q&A

- "Why not Shopify?" → "Shopify assumes you have a product. We start from an idea."
- "How do you make money?" → "7% on every transaction. Plus Stripe DUM Points purchases."
- "Why Solana?" → "On-chain rewards create a cross-business loyalty network that's verifiable and transferable."
- "What about AI hallucination?" → "The assistant only knows what's in the database — real offers, real prices. It's constrained by design."
- "Is this real?" → "Open any project page and try to buy something. It's Stripe test mode, but the whole flow works."
