## Outreach Targets by Channel

### Whatnot (priority — direct competitors)
- Search by category: trading cards, sneakers, vintage, collectibles, toys
- Sort by "recently live" to find active sellers
- Target sellers with 100-5,000 followers (big enough to sell, small enough to want alternatives)
- Look for sellers who complain about fees in their stream titles or socials

### TikTok Shop
- Search #TikTokShop, #SmallBusinessTikTok, #LiveSelling
- Target accounts with 1K-50K followers actively selling products
- Avoid pure content creators — focus on people moving product

### eBay Live
- New feature, sellers are experimenting — they're open to alternatives
- Search eBay Live categories, note seller usernames, find them on Instagram/TikTok for DM

### Local Service Businesses (NJ/NY metro first)
- Google Maps search: auto detailing, pet grooming, personal training, tutoring, home cleaning
- Target businesses with 4+ star ratings and 20+ reviews
- These businesses already pay for visibility — DUM Club replaces that spend
