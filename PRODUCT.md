# DUM CLUB — PRODUCT DIRECTION

## CORE IDENTITY
DUM Club is a marketplace where businesses and creators sell prepaid service credits that customers can use, redeem, or transfer.

It is NOT a trading platform or investment app.

Everything in the product, copy, UX, and system design must feel like buying and using services — not speculating on assets.

## PRODUCT PRIORITY
Focus on real-world execution first. Do not optimize for hype. Do not optimize for speculative token behavior. Do not build future layers before proving the base model works.

## LAYERS (STRICT ORDER — DO NOT MIX)
- Layer 1 (NOW): Real businesses + simple services
- Layer 2 (NEXT): AI/creator services
- Layer 3 (FUTURE): DUM Vault — Time Capsules / memory / identity layer

Focus entirely on Layer 1 until it is proven with real users and real redemptions.

## LAYER 1 EXAMPLES
- Pizza shop
- Barber
- Gym
- Personal trainer
- Meal prep service
- Local service provider
- Creator with a clearly defined digital service

These businesses sell usable service credits, not speculative tokens.

## CREDIT MODEL
Credits represent access to a real-world or digital service.

Supply must be based on real business capacity, not arbitrary fixed-supply token logic.

Examples:
- 1 credit = 1 pizza
- 1 credit = 1 haircut
- 1 credit = 1 class
- 1 credit = 1 consultation
- 1 credit = full access to a defined digital program

## SERVICE TYPES

### Physical services
Examples:
- pizza
- haircut
- in-person class
- meal

Rules:
- 1:1 delivery
- tied to real operational capacity
- must clearly match what can actually be fulfilled

### Scalable services
Examples:
- trainer plan
- coaching program
- digital course
- AI-based creator service

Rules:
- 1:many delivery is allowed
- the service must still be clearly defined
- each credit must correspond to a specific access right or benefit

Example:
- Program: 8-week fat loss plan
- 1 credit = full access
- Capacity = 100 users

## LANGUAGE RULES (ENFORCE EVERYWHERE)
Replace speculative trading language in the UI, product copy, and flows.

Use:
- "Get Credits"
- "Use Credits"
- "Redeem"
- "Transfer Credits"
- "Exchange Access"
- "Usage"
- "Redemption"
- "Demand"
- "Availability"
- "Activity"

Avoid:
- "Buy" when it sounds like speculation
- "Sell" when it sounds like trading
- "Trade" when it sounds like speculation
- "Pump"
- "Moon"
- "Profit"
- "ROI"
- "Price goes up"
- "Market cap" as a primary user-facing concept
- gain/loss style investment language

Users get credits, redeem services, and transfer unused credits. Never frame the product around profit or speculation.

## LEGAL AND POSITIONING RULES
- No investment framing
- No promise or implication of financial upside
- Credits are utility only
- DUM token is platform utility only
- Value must always be framed as usage-driven
- If a feature creates an investment-like user experience, remove it or redesign it

## DUM TOKEN UTILITY
DUM token may be used for:
- business access or staking tiers
- promotion or visibility inside the platform
- fee routing
- platform-level utility
- optional reward mechanics tied to activity

DUM must never be framed as a profit vehicle.

## LIQUIDITY / TRANSFER MODEL
The system allows unused credits to be transferred or exchanged because that improves flexibility for real customers.

This flexibility must be framed as:
- convenience
- customer flexibility
- better utilization
- business demand visibility

It must NOT be framed as:
- speculation
- flipping
- trading gains
- investing

## UI RULES
User-facing UI should emphasize:
- what the credit gets you
- redemption status
- demand
- usage
- availability
- business details
- fulfillment clarity
- trust

Do NOT emphasize:
- gain percentages
- speculative charts
- hype language
- investment-style dashboards

The platform should feel closer to:
- Shopify
- gift cards
- prepaid service access
- bookings
- memberships

It should NOT feel like:
- a stock terminal
- a meme coin app
- a trading exchange

## BUILD ORDER (DO NOT CHANGE)
1. Fee router smart contract
2. Credit mint system
3. Business staking / access system
4. Basic transfer / exchange mechanism
5. Marketplace and dashboard UI
6. Redemption and fulfillment flow

## MVP GOAL (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
To prove the system, the MVP must achieve:
- 3 to 5 real local businesses or creators
- real users
- 1 real credit issued
- 1 real redemption
- 1 real transfer

Do not scale before this is working in the real world.

## DECISION FILTER
When making any feature, UI, naming, or flow decision, ask:

1. Does this help a real business sell real service credits?
2. Does this help a real customer understand, buy, redeem, or transfer service access?
3. Does this feel useful and trustworthy?
4. Does this avoid feeling like speculation or investing?

If the answer to #4 is no, redesign it.

## FUTURE LAYERS (DO NOT BUILD YET)
Layer 2:
- AI-generated services
- creator tools
- digital/AI offerings built on the same credit model

Layer 3:
- DUM Vault
- Time Capsules
- memory / identity / long-term storage systems

These can come later. Do not let them distract from proving Layer 1.

## FINAL RULE
If any feature, copy, workflow, or interface makes DUM Club feel like investing, trading, or speculation, remove it or redesign it. Focus on simplicity, trust, fulfillment, and real-world use.
